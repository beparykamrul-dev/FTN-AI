# FTN Brand Asset Policy

The approved primary identity is the FTN Signature Orbital Emblem.

## Asset hierarchy
1. Vector/flat master — canonical production asset.
2. Monochrome variants — accessibility, engraving, low-bandwidth and constrained UI.
3. Light/dark background variants — presentation and product surfaces.
4. 3D/glossy render — marketing/presentation only.

## Rules
- Preserve the FTN monogram geometry.
- Do not add service names to the primary mark.
- Do not place the motto inside the primary logo artwork.
- Do not introduce unrelated decorative symbols.
- Maintain sufficient contrast at small sizes.
- Generate raster sizes from the vector master rather than editing individual raster copies.
