# FTN ISP Platform — Enterprise Capability Positioning

## Product pillars

1. Unified ISP Operations: billing, customer care, NOC, inventory, topology, monitoring and support in one control plane.
2. Real-time Operations: authenticated WebSocket event delivery for service, billing, incident and device telemetry events.
3. Multi-Panel Operations: dedicated Super Admin, ISP Admin, Billing, NOC, Engineer, Support, Reseller, Partner, Audit and AI Operations workspaces.
4. Intelligent Assurance: AI-assisted incident correlation, diagnosis, recovery planning and post-recovery verification.
5. Zero-Trust Device Management: infrastructure access stays server-side behind RBAC, approval gates and audited adapters.
6. Customer Digital Experience: Android/web self-service for account, service, billing, payments, support, speed testing and authorized service status.
7. Multi-Vendor Network Operations: adapter architecture for MikroTik, OLT/ONU, switches, servers and telemetry sources without coupling the customer app to vendor credentials.

## Differentiators to demonstrate with evidence

- Real-time operational event stream
- Customer-to-service-to-network relationship visibility
- AI recommendation with human approval for privileged changes
- Automatic verification and rollback workflow
- Role-specific operational panels
- Unified billing + network support context
- Auditable payment and recovery workflows
- Extensible device-adapter architecture

## Marketing guardrail

Do not claim carrier-grade, autonomous, zero-downtime, SLA, AI accuracy, security certification, or vendor compatibility unless the corresponding production test, certification, or integration evidence exists.

## Suggested positioning

"FTN ISP Platform unifies customer experience, billing, NOC operations and intelligent network assurance behind one secure control plane."
